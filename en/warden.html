Modular personal equipment
EquipmentResearch

A modular system of personal equipment. Protection, clothing, footwear, helmets, load-bearing gear, and support modules are developed as one complete set: a comfortable fit, freedom of movement, and carefully balanced load distribution.

Request a presentation

A complete set that works together

How the elements work together matters: whether armor restricts movement, how weight is distributed, and how quickly the wearer can access what they need.

WARDEN considers these questions from the start of development. Exoskeleton modules, power, and climate control systems extend the equipment for different working conditions.
