BACKEYE is an advanced external view system for military ground vehicles and remotely operated robotic platforms.

Send request

About the project

At the heart of the project is a compact unmanned aircraft with a camera, designed to provide an additional viewpoint on the vehicle and its surroundings.

The system combines an aerial video module, an onboard docking station, display tools, and software integration with the Darlein Defense digital environment.

BACKEYE supplements the vehicle’s onboard cameras with a third-person view, allowing the operator to assess the vehicle’s position, its dimensions, and surrounding objects in a single interface.

BACKEYE is an additional viewpoint that becomes part of the vehicle.

See the whole vehicle

A new perspective on ground vehicle control

Onboard cameras and viewing devices show the surroundings from points mounted directly on the vehicle’s hull.

Such images do not always make it possible to see the platform itself, its dimensions, and its position relative to surrounding objects at the same time.

BACKEYE explores the concept of an additional external view using a compact aerial video module.

The resulting image can supplement data from onboard cameras and the vehicle’s technical readings, giving a clearer picture of its position.

Instead of several independent information sources — a single digital observation environment.

Movement, inspection, and work

Three BACKEYE use scenarios
01 / 03

Clearance checks

The whole vehicle in view

An additional external angle makes it possible to visually assess the vehicle’s position relative to surrounding objects.

The concept is aimed at improving visibility when working with large and remotely operated platforms.

02 / 03

Movement monitoring

External view as a supplement to onboard cameras

The operator gets an additional image source to monitor the vehicle’s position and changes in its surroundings.

03 / 03

External technical inspection

Look at the vehicle from the outside

The aerial video module can be considered a tool for remote visual inspection of the vehicle’s accessible exterior elements.

The images obtained are used to assess the visible condition of the equipment and for subsequent technical analysis.
