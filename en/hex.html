Communications for people, vehicles, and sensors
NetworkResearch

A family of networking devices for wearable electronics, vehicles, unmanned platforms, and sensors. A distributed mesh network in which nodes transmit their own data and help relay data from other participants.

One network for the whole team

People and vehicles move, connections change, and channel capacities vary.

HEX uses available paths and updates routes as the network changes. The network is designed to work without requiring a permanent central node.
