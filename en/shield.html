About the project

The project envisages developing four independent areas of armament and protection, united by a shared engineering philosophy, unified electronic components, and digital infrastructure.

Each system has its own purpose, hardware architecture, and testing requirements.

SHIELD is the technological foundation of an advanced family of combat and protection systems.

Four areas. Different technologies

A family of remotely operated combat modules with machine gun armament for integration with military vehicles, robotic platforms, and fixed installations.

This area envisages developing various design versions with a unified electronic architecture, diagnostic tools, and software interfaces.

A family of military systems based on modular transport and launch containers with unmanned aerial vehicles.

The project envisages various container system configurations, unified hardware components, and integration into the Darlein Defense digital infrastructure.

A promising development area: specialized active protection systems for military vehicles.

The project combines research into protective technologies, hardware components, and system integration with various classes of armored vehicles.

A research area focused on developing military directed energy systems based on laser technologies.

The project envisages developing a specialized hardware platform, power components, optoelectronic systems, and technical support infrastructure.

Different systems. One technology ecosystem

SHIELD brings four independent areas of military technology together under one brand.

Common development standards, digital interfaces, electronic components, and modularity principles form the basis for developing advanced systems.

Integration with Darlein Defense software and hardware projects envisages compatibility at the level of permitted information services, technical diagnostics, and equipment lifecycle management.
