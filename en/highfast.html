HIGHFAST

Vertical takeoff. Protected mobility. A new dimension in logistics
Military transport aviation systemsConcept development

HIGHFAST is an advanced multipurpose vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) aviation platform designed for personnel transport, cargo delivery, medical evacuation, and auxiliary military logistics tasks.

Send request

About the project

The project explores the creation of a family of aircraft that combine vertical takeoff capability, a modular transport architecture, safety enhancement features, and digital integration with the Darlein Defense ecosystem.

The core idea of HIGHFAST is to provide mobility where the use of conventional ground transport infrastructure is difficult or impossible.

Mobility without dependence on roads

A new architecture for military transport aviation

Ground transportation depends on the condition of roads, bridges, terrain, and route availability. In difficult conditions, this can limit the timely delivery of people, equipment, and essential resources.

HIGHFAST explores an alternative approach to transport mobility.

Vertical takeoff and landing make it possible to consider using confined landing sites, and the modular architecture makes it possible to adapt the transport platform to different categories of transport.

The project is built on three principles: mobility, safety, and versatility.

One platform. Multiple roles

An advanced transport configuration for moving service members between equipped and suitable temporary landing sites.

Development includes research into the passenger compartment, restraint systems, emergency egress, and the safety of people on board.

Transport of food, water, medical supplies, spare parts, communications equipment, and other essential materiel.

The modular cargo bay provides for various options for placing and securing cargo.

A specialized configuration for transporting the wounded and injured.

Provision is made for accommodating stretchers, medical equipment, and accompanying personnel, depending on the size and class of the platform.

Research into comprehensive measures to improve flight safety and passenger protection.

Priority areas include technical condition monitoring, detection of hazardous conditions, crew alerting, redundancy of critical systems, and passive protection measures.

The option of developing separate platform variants to meet the requirements of government and agency customers.

The modular architecture provides for adapting interior equipment, communications equipment, and information systems.

A prospective development line for unmanned cargo variants with automated flight functions, technical condition checks, and remote monitoring capability.

Safety as the foundation of the design

Protecting people is not an optional extra

HIGHFAST takes a comprehensive approach to the safety of passengers, equipment, and flight.

Main research areas
  • Redundancy of critical systems
  • Real-time technical condition monitoring
  • Hazard warning systems
  • Passive protection of the passenger compartment
  • Safe cargo placement and securing
  • Emergency egress equipment
  • Fault diagnostics and prediction

Specific technical solutions and their effectiveness are to be determined by the results of engineering research and testing.
