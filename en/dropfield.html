About the project

A single digital platform brings together manufacturers, suppliers, stores, and buyers.

From a single item of equipment to complete outfitting — all in one ecosystem.

One product. Multiple offers

A single catalog for the military gear and equipment market

The military products market spans thousands of items from different manufacturers and suppliers.

Buyers have to compare specifications, check equipment compatibility, and look for suitable offers on their own.

DROPFIELD builds a single standardized catalog in which one model brings together offers from different sellers, specifications, variants, and purchase terms.

Compare offers, choose the right equipment, and build your own loadouts without having to search for everything across different platforms.

What the marketplace includes

Six services of one military ecosystem

A single marketplace for military equipment, tactical gear, specialized electronics, unmanned systems, and hardware from verified suppliers.

A space for users to buy and sell used gear, equipment, and hardware that is legal to trade.

Custom manufacturing and modification of equipment, accessories, and specialized items to the customer’s requirements.

A digital configurator for building equipment loadouts, trying them on visually on an avatar, and checking the compatibility of individual items.

Create, save, and share your own gear setups. Explore ready-made loadouts and choose equipment for specific tasks.

Tools for centralized organizational procurement, supplier interaction, paperwork, and repeat purchases.

From buying a single item of equipment to fully outfitting a unit or organization. DROPFIELD brings the key tools of specialized trade together in one digital environment.

The first product to launch the ecosystem

DROPFIELD — the commercial platform of the Darlein Defense ecosystem

Darlein Defense is a parent technology brand that brings together promising projects in defense technology, software, robotics, unmanned systems, and specialized equipment.

DROPFIELD becomes the commerce arm of this ecosystem — a digital infrastructure connecting developers, manufacturers, suppliers, and end buyers of military products.

The platform’s development plan includes expanding tools for corporate and government customers, integrating external services, and building a single environment for specialized trade.
