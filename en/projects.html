Products are designed to be compatible

AGATA collects data and status from vehicles, communications, and equipment. HEX connects people and machines into one network, ECHO provides observations, and ELZA and EVA help with analysis and work.

Each product can be used on its own. Together they form a shared digital environment with common interfaces and a common approach to data.
