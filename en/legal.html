1. General provisions

  1. This Policy sets out the procedure for processing personal data and the measures to ensure its security at DARLEIN DEFENSE LLC (the “Operator”) in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 “On Personal Data”.
  2. The Policy applies to all personal data that the Operator receives from site visitors, including data sent through contact, application, and job application forms.
  3. The current version of the Policy is published on the site. The revision date is shown in the document header.

2. Data processed

  1. Full name, organization name, email address, and the content of the message.
  2. For job applications — information voluntarily provided by the applicant in the CV and cover letter.
  3. Technical web analytics data: cookies, IP address, browser and device information. Special categories of data are not processed.

3. Purposes of processing

  1. Responding to messages, partner and vendor applications, product requests, and product launch notifications.
  2. Reviewing job applications.
  3. Improving the site based on anonymized statistics.
  4. Sending marketing and informational emails — only with the subject’s separate consent.

4. Legal grounds

  1. The consent of the personal data subject, given when submitting a form.
  2. Separate consent to receive marketing and informational emails (Article 18 of Federal Law No. 38-FZ of 13.03.2006 “On Advertising”).
  3. The need to process data to conclude and perform a contract initiated by the subject.

5. Procedure and conditions of processing

  1. Processing is carried out with and without automation. The collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, and retrieval of personal data are carried out using databases located in the Russian Federation: users’ data is stored on servers in Russia. Necessary cookies ensure the operation of the site; analytics cookies are used only after the user gives consent in the banner on the site; for details, see the Cookie Policy. Marketing and informational emails are sent only with a separate mailing consent, and the user may opt out of them at any time.
  2. The Operator does not disclose personal data or transfer it to third parties, except as required by the laws of the Russian Federation.
  3. Data is stored until the purposes of processing are achieved or consent is withdrawn, unless otherwise required by law.

6. Rights of the subject

  1. The subject may obtain information about the processing of their data, request its clarification, blocking, or destruction, and withdraw consent.
  2. Requests are sent to the Operator’s email address given in section 8. The response time is as required by law.

7. Security measures

  1. The Operator applies organizational and technical measures: access control, encryption of communication channels, media accounting, and staff training.

1. General provisions

  1. This Policy describes which cookies and similar technologies the Darlein Defense website (the “site”) uses, what they are used for, and how to manage them. The site operator is DARLEIN DEFENSE LLC (the “Operator”).
  2. Cookies are small text files that a site stores in a visitor’s browser. Similar technologies include browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). Hereinafter, all of them are referred to as cookies.
  3. This Policy supplements the Personal Data Processing Policy. Data obtained through cookies is processed under its terms.

2. Cookies used

  1. Necessary. They ensure the operation of the site: they remember the choice made in the cookie banner and that the loading screen has already been shown in the current session. They are used without separate consent, since the site does not work correctly without them. No personal data is stored in them.
  2. Analytics. They help understand how visitors use the site and improve it: information about pages viewed, the referral source, browser and device, and IP address. They are used only after the visitor gives consent by clicking “Accept” in the cookie banner.
  3. Advertising cookies and cookies for displaying personalized advertising are not used on the site.

3. Third-party services

  1. Analytics cookies may be set by web analytics services that the Operator connects to the site. A list of such services is provided in this Policy when they are connected.
  2. Data obtained through cookies is stored on servers in the Russian Federation and is not transferred to third parties, except as required by the laws of the Russian Federation.

4. Retention period

  1. The choice made in the cookie banner is stored in the browser until the visitor clears the site data. The loading screen flag is deleted when the tab is closed.
  2. Analytics cookies are stored for no longer than 12 months.

5. How to manage cookies

  1. On the first visit, the site shows a banner: “Accept” allows all cookies, and “Necessary only” allows only the cookies without which the site does not work.
  2. The decision can be changed by clearing the site data in the browser: the banner will appear again. Cookies can also be blocked or deleted in the browser settings; if necessary cookies are disabled, some site features may not work correctly.
