Two routesPartner · Vendor

Partners

We consider bringing teams on board, acquiring technology and rights, integrating developments into our products, and manufacturing components. A vendor sells on the DROPFIELD marketplace. These are different relationship models, so their terms are described separately. Choose a route before you read on.

Ecosystem partner

Your product gets a standard, a channel, and an umbrella brand. Its independence and the character of the sub-brand remain yours.

Who is a fit

  • Engineering teams and manufacturers with a product at the prototype stage or beyond in one of the seven areas of the ecosystem.
  • Teams missing one of six elements: funding, product expertise, design, production, team, or market access.

What you get

  • Common requirements for quality, design, and compatibility — and help meeting them.
  • Market entry under an umbrella brand: marketing, B2B and B2G procedures, legal support.
  • Compatibility with the AGATA platform and other products through an open specification.

What is required

  • Readiness to work to one quality standard and to undergo testing.
  • Transparency about development rights and the origin of components.
  • Compliance with restrictions: no tasks that require permits without having them.

What remains yours

The independence of your product and the character of your sub-brand. The ecosystem sets the standard and the environment; it does not replace your team or your brand. Terms on rights and commerce are set out in the agreement and are not published.

How it works

Four steps

Company, product or area, contact. We reply within five business days.

DROPFIELD vendor

A verified status, one product card standard, and access to a professional audience. Your brand stays yours in the storefront.

Who is a fit

  • Manufacturers and authorized suppliers of equipment, communications gear, optics, wearable electronics, protective gear, tools, and software services.
  • Companies with legally clean product origin and readiness to take on support obligations.

What you get

  • A verified vendor status, visible on every product card.
  • One product card standard: your products read as clearly as any other seller’s and are included in comparisons.
  • Access to a professional audience and customer organizations through one channel.

What is required

  • Filling in product cards to the marketplace standard: required fields, specifications, photos.
  • Passing verification: legal status, product origin, warranty and support terms.
  • Compliance with marketplace rules and product category requirements.

What remains yours

Your brand in the storefront, pricing within marketplace rules, and the warranty relationship with the buyer. Commission and contract terms are discussed individually and are not published.

How it works

Three steps
01Application

Company, product categories, contact. We reply within three business days.

02Verification

Documents, product origin, support obligations. Verification status.

03Onboarding

Contract, access to the vendor account, uploading product cards to the standard, publication.
