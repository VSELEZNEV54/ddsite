SEAL

Unmanned maritime platforms
USVResearch

A family of unmanned maritime platforms for observation, communications, transport, and specialized tasks. Shared control systems and modular equipment make it possible to create different versions within one product line.

Many tasks. One maritime platform

The vessel, navigation, communications, and payload need to work together.

SEAL unites them through a common hardware and software foundation: proven solutions are reused, and the approach to maintenance stays consistent.
