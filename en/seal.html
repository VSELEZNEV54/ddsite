SEAL

Maritime robotics. A unified platform. Different tasks
Unmanned maritime platformsResearch

SEAL is an advanced family of modular unmanned maritime platforms from Darlein Defense, designed for remote observation, equipment transport, marine environment monitoring, and specialized tasks.

Send request

About the project

The project brings together various classes of surface vessels based on a unified electronic architecture, compatible software components, and modular equipment.

SEAL is built on common principles of development, digital control, maintenance, and integration of additional equipment.

From a compact unmanned boat to a large multifunctional platform, SEAL forms the technology foundation for the development of maritime robotics.

Many tasks. One maritime platform

Modularity instead of many independent developments

Building maritime robotic systems requires integrating the hull, power plant, navigation equipment, sensors, communications modules, and software.

Developing each new configuration from scratch increases the complexity of production, testing, and subsequent maintenance.

SEAL takes a unified approach.

Common electronic components, standardized interfaces, and compatible software tools form a single technology foundation for different variants of maritime platforms.

The specific configuration is determined by the vessel’s purpose, equipment requirements, and operating conditions.

One architecture. A family of maritime solutions.

Open architecture
