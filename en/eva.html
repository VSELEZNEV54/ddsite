EVA

Intelligence that sees the world with you
Software and AIResearch

EVA is a next-generation multimodal AI assistant that combines computer vision, voice interaction, augmented reality, and contextual analysis of the surrounding environment.

Send request

About the project

The system uses camera images, speech, data from connected devices, documentation, and available digital information to understand what is happening and help the user in real time.

EVA is being designed to run on smartphones, tablets, personal computers, and compatible wearable devices, including AR displays.

From everyday interaction with technology to professional work with complex equipment, EVA becomes an intelligent interface between people, information, and the world around them.

It doesn’t just answer. It understands context

Artificial intelligence that perceives the world around it

Most digital assistants interact with users mainly through text or voice. They can answer questions but often lack sufficient information about physical objects, the user’s actions, and events taking place around them.

EVA combines multiple sources of information into a single contextual model.

The camera makes it possible to analyze visible objects. The microphone enables natural dialogue. Device data helps take their status into account. Documentation and knowledge bases provide specialized information.

As a result, users will be able not only to ask questions but also to show objects, receive explanations, carry out step-by-step tasks, and interact with digital tools without constantly switching between apps.

Show. Ask. Act.

From observation to action

One continuous process of interaction with AI
01Perception

EVA receives information from the camera, the microphone, and authorized connected sources.

02Understanding

AI determines what the request is about, analyzes available data, and takes the context of the current task into account.

03Research

When necessary, the system refers to documentation, local materials, or connected information resources.

04Recommendation

EVA produces an answer, an explanation, a visual cue, or a sequence of suggested actions.

05Interaction

The user clarifies the task, continues the dialogue, or confirms execution of an available operation.

06Continuation

Context is retained within an authorized session, making it possible to continue work without explaining the whole task again.

Capabilities

An assistant that understands your task
01 / 041
Multimodal interaction

Voice, images, text, documentation, and device data come together in a single interface.

Users choose the most convenient way to interact depending on the task at hand.

02 / 042
Contextual memory

EVA is designed to retain the permitted context of the dialogue, ongoing tasks, and materials used.

This makes it possible to continue sequential work and receive answers that take previously provided information into account.

03 / 043
Verifiable recommendations

When using documentation and external information sources, EVA must provide links to the materials on which an answer is based.

When information is insufficient, the system asks for clarification and indicates the limitations of its conclusions.

04 / 044
Form-factor independence

One assistant concept for different devices: smartphones, tablets, computers, and compatible wearable systems.

Open architecture. New capabilities

An intelligent interface for your applications and devices

EVA is being designed as an extensible system capable of interacting with third-party software solutions and compatible hardware.

Software interfaces are planned for integrating external services, corporate systems, and specialized tools.

The architecture may include
  • APIs for connecting applications and services
  • Integration of external knowledge bases
  • Support for various AI models
  • Connection of compatible devices and sensors
  • Configurable tools and scenarios
  • Permission and access management
  • The option to deploy individual components locally

EVA is meant to evolve not as an isolated application but as an intelligent software layer that can be used in a variety of digital products.

People and technology. A single interface

EVA — an intelligent component of the Darlein Defense ecosystem

EVA is being developed both as a standalone AI product and as the interface between people and Darlein Defense digital systems.

Integration with WARDEN provides for the use of compatible wearable devices, displays, and sensor components.

Interaction with AGATA will provide access to authorized communication tools, map information, and workspaces through a contextual interface.

Integration with ELZA provides for using analytical results and verifiable information materials to expand the assistant’s capabilities.

Together, these areas form a single concept: information, analytics, and interaction with technology become accessible through a natural interface.
