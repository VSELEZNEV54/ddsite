EVA

AI assistant for working with equipment
AIResearch

An AI assistant for working with equipment and physical objects. It combines camera input, voice, sensor data, and documentation to give people relevant guidance while they carry out a task.

Request a presentation

Show. Ask. Keep working

To help a specialist, an assistant needs to know which component is in front of them, what has already been done, and what has changed.

EVA keeps track of that sequence, connects observations with instructions, and removes the need to switch between the job and a search for reference material.
