AGATA

Management, data, and coordination
ManagementIn development · available on request

A software platform for coordinating people, equipment, and resources. Maps, communications, tasks, video, and equipment data come together in one interface, so the team can see what is happening and work from the same information.

Request a presentation

Manage it all. In one system

When data is spread across applications, time is spent finding, checking, and passing on information.

In AGATA, information is linked to specific assets and tasks: equipment has a status and history, a task has people and resources, and an event has a source. The platform is in development: module readiness is announced separately, and demonstrations are available on request.

Offline access

Core functions and saved data remain available locally. When connectivity returns, the data syncs.

Sharing between teams

Independent servers exchange approved data. Each organization retains its own users and access rules.

More tools

APIs, SDKs, and plugins add devices, data, and functions. An extension catalog tracks versions and permissions.

People, equipment, and tasks. Together

AGATA is the digital foundation of the Darlein Defense ecosystem: vehicles, communications, sensors, and equipment connect to one system.

The platform is designed for the cloud, your own server, and isolated infrastructure. The team chooses the deployment model and set of modules.
