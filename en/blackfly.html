BLACKFLY

A unified technology platform for unmanned systems
Unmanned aerial systemsResearch

BLACKFLY is Darlein Defense’s modular hardware and software ecosystem for building, integrating, configuring, and maintaining unmanned platforms.

Send request

About the project

The project brings together unified electronic components, software, computing modules, sensors, development tools, and digital infrastructure for managing the life cycle of unmanned systems.

BLACKFLY is built on common principles of architecture, compatibility, and reuse of engineering solutions.

From an experimental prototype to a family of production models — BLACKFLY is meant to reduce the need to develop the same components over and over and to create a common technology foundation for a range of unmanned platforms.

Your platform. Our technology foundation

Different vehicles. One architecture

Developing an unmanned system requires combining many hardware and software components.

Even when using off-the-shelf solutions, engineers have to deal with hardware compatibility, software integration, diagnostics, and support for different configurations.

BLACKFLY offers a systematic approach.

Instead of creating a separate electronic architecture for each new product, it establishes a single modular platform with documented interfaces, compatible components, and configuration management tools.

This lets engineering resources focus on developing the product itself, building on a reusable technology foundation.

Our own unmanned platforms

BLACKFLY — the technology core of Darlein Defense’s unmanned systems area

Beyond developing a universal component ecosystem, Darlein Defense sees BLACKFLY as the technology foundation for future families of its own unmanned vehicles.

Various classes of platforms are envisioned within this area.

01 / 04

SCOUT

Compact unmanned systems for observation, environmental research, and capturing visual data.

02 / 04

MICRONE

Miniature unmanned platforms with an emphasis on compactness, portability, and the integration of small-form-factor electronics.

03 / 04

LITTLEONE

Lightweight multipurpose unmanned platforms with a modular architecture and the ability to mount compatible equipment.

04 / 04

HEAVYONE

A prospective line of larger unmanned platforms, with expanded hardware integration capabilities and a scalable hardware architecture.

A common technology foundation makes it possible to treat these families as parts of one engineering ecosystem rather than as independent projects.
