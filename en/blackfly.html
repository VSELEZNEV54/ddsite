BLACKFLY

Components for unmanned platforms
UASResearch

A modular ecosystem for building unmanned platforms. Control, power, communications, observation, navigation, and computing components are combined with software tools that configure them and enable them to work together.

Request a presentation

Your platform. Our components

When developing a new vehicle, much of the effort goes into getting equipment to work together.

BLACKFLY puts compatibility at the heart of the product: the team selects modules, checks the configuration, and saves its digital description. Proven solutions carry over into later designs.
