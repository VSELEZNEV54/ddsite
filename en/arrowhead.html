ARROWHEAD

A family of unmanned interceptors
Air defenseResearch

A family of containerized unmanned interceptors. The aircraft is considered together with its production, storage, maintenance, and digital records. The product line includes two classes: the baseline ARROWHEAD and the high-speed SPEAR.

Request a presentation

Two classes. One system

Consistent manufacturing, a clear view of technical condition, and total lifecycle cost are central to the family’s development.

These requirements are part of the product from the outset. Shared container infrastructure and digital integration connect the two classes while preserving their distinct roles.
