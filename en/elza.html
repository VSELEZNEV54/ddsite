About the project

The platform combines artificial intelligence, big data processing, graph analysis, geospatial tools, and predictive modeling.

ELZA is designed for automated collection and cross-referencing of information, discovering hidden relationships, testing hypotheses, identifying risks, and producing analytical conclusions that can be checked against primary sources.

From personal research to the analytical infrastructure of a large organization, ELZA helps turn scattered data into structured knowledge.

See connections. Understand events

The world is made up of interconnected processes. ELZA helps you see them as a whole

Vast amounts of information appear every day: news, research, government documents, financial indicators, statistics, satellite data, and publications in digital sources.

Events that seem unrelated can be parts of a single process. Changes in legislation affect companies. Economic decisions reshape markets. Technological developments transform industries. Local events can trigger far-reaching consequences.

Traditional analysis requires searching dozens of sources, cross-checking facts by hand, and constantly tracking changes.

ELZA creates a unified analytical environment in which data is automatically structured, cross-referenced, and transformed into an explorable model of what is happening.

Users get more than an answer to a question: they gain the ability to understand the causes of events, explore relationships, and assess how the situation may develop.

What is happening and why

Six key tools of the analytics platform

ELZA provides for connecting open sources, information resources, documents, databases, and organizations’ internal systems.

The platform will be able to automatically extract information, identify key entities, remove duplicates, and combine scattered material into structured datasets.

Key capabilities
  • Monitoring of news, publications, and open data.
  • Analysis of documents, spreadsheets, and text materials.
  • Connection of corporate databases via API.
  • Automatic classification and organization of information.
  • Processing of multilingual sources.
  • Identification of events, objects, organizations, and people.
  • Updating of analytical data as new information arrives.

ELZA builds a dynamic model of the relationships between events, organizations, people, objects, resources, and processes.

An interactive graph lets users explore direct and indirect connections, find common factors, and trace how relationships change over time.

Key capabilities
  • Visualization of relationships between objects.
  • Automatic detection of potential connections.
  • Analysis of event chains and dependencies.
  • Consolidation of information from multiple sources.
  • Display of historical changes.
  • Identification of information gaps and contradictions.
  • Navigation from each graph element to the supporting data.

The built-in AI assistant lets users explore complex questions in natural language.

The user formulates a task, and ELZA searches for relevant information, cross-references material, highlights significant facts, and produces a structured analytical result.

Key capabilities
  • Exploration of questions in natural language.
  • Search and cross-referencing of information from different sources.
  • Automatic generation of analytical summaries.
  • Hypothesis testing and search for counterarguments.
  • Detection of conflicting statements.
  • Report generation with source citations.
  • Separation of confirmed facts, interpretations, and assumptions.

ELZA lets users explore how events might unfold based on historical data, identified patterns, and defined conditions.

Instead of a single categorical forecast, the system generates alternative scenarios and shows the factors that could influence whether they materialize.

Key capabilities
  • Analysis of historical dynamics.
  • Identification of trends and changes.
  • Modeling of alternative scenarios.
  • Analysis of the impact of individual factors.
  • Assessment of risks and uncertainty.
  • Comparison of scenarios against defined criteria.
  • Revision of assessments as new data arrives.

Users will be able to set up their own analytical focus areas and track events, organizations, markets, technologies, and indicators of interest.

ELZA analyzes incoming data and highlights changes that may matter for the selected task.

Key capabilities
  • Personal watchlists.
  • Tracking of events and objects.
  • Monitoring of changes in key indicators.
  • Automatic anomaly detection.
  • Configurable notifications.
  • History of changes and events.
  • Periodic analytical summaries.

ELZA links events to their geographic location and time attributes.

Interactive maps and timelines make it possible to explore how processes develop, compare events across regions, and identify spatiotemporal patterns.

Key capabilities
  • Display of events on an interactive map.
  • Analysis of the geographic distribution of data.
  • Timelines and event history.
  • Comparison of information by region.
  • Spatial filters.
  • Overlay of multiple information layers.
  • Analysis of process dynamics over time.

From question to analytical result

The full research cycle in a single interface
01Defining the task

The user asks a question, selects a subject of research, or formulates an analytical hypothesis.

02Collecting information

ELZA queries available sources, extracts relevant material, and builds the information base for the research.

03Structuring the data

The system identifies events, objects, relationships, and temporal and geographic attributes, combining them into a single model.

04Intelligent analysis

AI cross-references information, tests hypotheses, and looks for contradictions, patterns, and potentially significant changes.

05Scenarios and conclusions

The platform produces analytical results, possible scenarios, and risk assessments, indicating the source data and limitations.

06Continuous updating

The research is saved. New data can automatically supplement it, change assessments, and trigger notifications.

Open architecture. Your own data. Your own tools

An analytics platform adaptable to any industry

ELZA is being designed as a modular software system that can integrate external sources, enterprise applications, and specialized analytical tools.

The architecture provides for
  • API for integrating external systems
  • Connection of your own data sources
  • Development of additional analytical modules
  • Configurable dashboards
  • User and access rights management
  • Cloud and on-premises deployment
  • The option to integrate different AI models

A single analytical core can be used to address tasks in economics, industry, finance, scientific research, the technology sector, public administration, and other fields.
