ELZA

Analytics and event research
AIResearch

An AI analytics platform for exploring events, processes, and risks. It compares sources, identifies relationships, and helps assess how a situation may develop. Every significant conclusion is linked to the data behind it.

See connections. Understand events

Political decisions affect markets, technologies reshape industries, and infrastructure conditions affect entire regions.

ELZA brings publications, statistics, documents, and observations into an evolving picture of events, participants, and factors that users can explore and verify.
