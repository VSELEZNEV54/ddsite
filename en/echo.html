Autonomous sensor systems
SensorsResearch

A family of autonomous sensor systems for land, air, and sea. Sensors, local processing, and data storage are combined so that observations from individual devices contribute to a shared picture of events.

Know what’s happening around you

A single signal provides a limited view of a situation.

More information emerges when observations from different sensors, neighboring nodes, and earlier periods are compared. ECHO preserves this history and assesses event reliability.
